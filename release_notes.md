## BetterRoads v1.2.0 - Advanced Feedback & UI Polish

This release introduces the much-requested advanced feedback system, new mobile usability improvements, and stronger anti-spam measures to keep community reporting high-quality.

**What's New in this Build:**
- **Advanced Feedback System:** Share suggestions, bugs, or general feedback directly from the app or website.
- **Silent Telemetry:** Automatically attaches device OS (Android/iOS) and timezone/location context to feedback to help us troubleshoot faster.
- **Spam Protection:** Integrated simple Math CAPTCHAs to block automated spam without frustrating users.
- **Profile Enforcement:** Users must now complete their profile (Name & Email) before submitting feedback, ensuring accountability.
- **Mobile Map Polish:** The Map Legend and Stats panels now collapse into sleek, floating circular buttons (FABs) on mobile devices to completely eliminate map overlap.
- **Polished UI:** Upgraded standard browser dropdowns to sleek, custom React menus for a premium feel.
